Week 1 — Foundations & Skills
01 Day

The AI Revolution: Why Now

Module 1 — The AI Landscape 30 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Understand what AI agents are and how they differ from simple chatbots
  • Identify the top 5 industries spending the most on AI services right now
  • Map out your personal "why" for building an AI business

Practice Tasks

  • Research 3 real businesses using AI agents (customer support, lead gen, or content). Write one sentence about each explaining what the AI does and why it saves money.
  • Open ChatGPT and ask it: "Act as a business consultant. What are the top 5 ways small businesses use AI agents in 2026?" Save the response.
  • Write your personal AI Business Goal Statement: "In 90 days, I will [specific outcome] by offering [type of AI service] to [type of client]."

Pro Tip: The AI services market is projected to reach $300B+ by 2027. You are not late — you are early. The businesses that need AI the most have not started looking yet.

02 Day

ChatGPT Power User

Module 2 — Your AI Toolkit 45 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Master the art of writing precise, high-value prompts that produce professional outputs
  • Understand temperature, system prompts, and custom instructions
  • Create your first reusable prompt template for a business use case

Practice Tasks

  • Write 5 prompts that follow the CRISP framework (Context, Role, Instructions, Specifics, Parameters). Test each one and rate the output quality from 1-10.
  • Create a "Client Email Responder" prompt template: give ChatGPT a business type, tone, and situation, and have it generate a professional email. Test with 3 different scenarios.
  • Build a Custom GPT instruction set (or system prompt) for a fictional restaurant that handles menu questions, booking requests, and complaints. Test 5 different customer messages.

Pro Tip: The difference between a £50/month AI user and a £5,000/month AI consultant is prompt engineering. Your prompts are your product — treat them like intellectual property.

03 Day

Visual AI: Leonardo & Image Generation

Module 2 — Your AI Toolkit 45 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Navigate Leonardo AI's interface and understand model selection for different styles
  • Write image prompts that produce consistent, brand-aligned visuals
  • Learn when to use AI images vs stock photos for client work

Practice Tasks

  • Create a set of 5 product images for a fictional skincare brand using Leonardo AI. Use a consistent style, colour palette, and lighting across all 5 images.
  • Generate 3 social media graphics (Instagram post dimensions) for a fictional coffee shop. Experiment with different models and compare results.
  • Document your best prompts in a "Visual AI Swipe File" — save the prompt, the settings used, and the output for future reference.

Pro Tip: Clients do not buy "AI images" — they buy consistent brand visuals delivered in hours instead of days. Always present your work as a brand asset, never as "something AI made."

04 Day

Voice & Audio: ElevenLabs Mastery

Module 2 — Your AI Toolkit 40 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Generate professional voiceovers using ElevenLabs with correct pacing and emotion
  • Understand voice cloning ethics and commercial licensing requirements
  • Identify 3 profitable voiceover services you can offer to clients

Practice Tasks

  • Write a 60-second script for a fictional podcast intro. Generate it with 3 different ElevenLabs voices. Compare tone, clarity, and naturalness.
  • Create a professional IVR (phone menu) recording: "Thank you for calling [Business]. Press 1 for Sales, Press 2 for Support..." using an appropriate voice and pacing.
  • Record yourself reading 30 seconds of text, then clone your voice in ElevenLabs. Compare the clone to the original and note the differences.

Pro Tip: Voiceover artists charge £200-500 per script. With ElevenLabs, you can offer "unlimited revisions, same-day turnaround" — something traditional voiceover cannot compete with.

05 Day

Build Your First AI Chatbot

Module 3 — Building AI Systems 60 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Build a functional AI chatbot using a no-code platform (Voiceflow, Botpress, or Tidio)
  • Design a conversation flow that handles FAQs, collects leads, and routes to humans when needed
  • Understand how to embed a chatbot on any website

Practice Tasks

  • Sign up for a free Voiceflow or Botpress account. Build a chatbot for a fictional dental practice that answers the top 10 patient questions (hours, services, pricing, booking).
  • Add a "lead capture" flow: when the bot cannot answer a question, it collects the visitor's name, email, and question, then confirms a team member will follow up.
  • Test your chatbot with 10 different questions (including tricky edge cases). Fix any responses that feel robotic or unhelpful. Screenshot the final conversation flows.

Pro Tip: A single AI chatbot can replace a £2,000/month customer service hire for a small business. That is not a feature — that is a transformation. Lead with the ROI when you pitch this to clients.

06 Day

Automation Workflows with Make & Zapier

Module 3 — Building AI Systems 60 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Understand the difference between Make (Integromat) and Zapier, and when to use each
  • Build a multi-step automation that connects 3+ tools with AI in the middle
  • Identify the most common automation workflows businesses pay for

Practice Tasks

  • Build a "Lead-to-Response" automation: when a new form submission arrives (Google Forms), use ChatGPT to draft a personalised response, then send it via Gmail. Test with 3 sample submissions.
  • Create a "Social Media Repurposer" workflow: take a blog post URL, use AI to generate 5 social media captions (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok), and save them to a Google Sheet.
  • Document both automations with screenshots in a "Portfolio Piece" format — show the workflow diagram, explain the business value, and estimate time saved per month.

Pro Tip: Businesses do not care about "automations" — they care about getting 10 hours back per week. Always frame your automations in terms of time saved and money recovered.

07 Day

AI Content Machine: Video & Social

Module 4 — AI Content Creation 60 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Create a professional AI-generated video using D-ID or HeyGen with a talking avatar
  • Build a complete social media content pack (post, carousel, video, caption) using only AI tools
  • Understand content creation as a recurring revenue service

Practice Tasks

  • Write a 45-second "Welcome to Our Business" script using ChatGPT. Generate an AI avatar video with D-ID or HeyGen. Add a voiceover from ElevenLabs if the avatar voice is not sufficient.
  • Create a "1 Week Content Pack" for a fictional personal trainer: 7 Instagram posts (image + caption), 2 Reels scripts, and 1 carousel. Use Leonardo for images and ChatGPT for copy.
  • Time yourself: how long did the full content pack take? Calculate what you would charge per pack (aim for £300-500). This is your first productised service.

Pro Tip: Content creation is the easiest AI service to sell because every business needs it, every business hates doing it, and AI makes it 10x faster. This alone can be a £3K/month business.

Week 2 — Business & Launch
08 Day

Build Your AI Portfolio

Module 4 — AI Content Creation 60 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Compile your best work from Days 1-7 into a professional portfolio
  • Learn how to present AI-generated work as case studies with measurable results
  • Create a "before and after" narrative that sells the transformation

Practice Tasks

  • Create 3 portfolio case studies from your practice work. Each should include: the problem, your AI solution, the tools used, visual samples, and an estimated ROI for the fictional client.
  • Build a simple portfolio page using Carrd, Notion, or Canva. Include your photo/brand, a one-line positioning statement, your 3 case studies, and a contact form.
  • Write your "AI Services Bio" (150 words max): who you help, what you do, and why AI-powered services deliver better results. This is your LinkedIn summary and cold email intro.

Pro Tip: You do not need 50 clients to have a portfolio. Three well-crafted case studies (even from fictional projects) are more convincing than a blank page. Action beats perfection.

09 Day

Finding Clients Who Need AI

Module 5 — Finding & Landing Clients 45 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Identify businesses that are actively struggling with problems AI can solve
  • Master the "AI Audit" approach — offering a free review that leads to paid work
  • Build a prospect list of 20 businesses you could approach this week

Practice Tasks

  • Pick a niche (e.g., estate agents, gyms, restaurants, coaches). Find 20 businesses in that niche on Google Maps or LinkedIn. For each, note one visible problem AI could fix (slow response time, no chatbot, generic content).
  • Write an "AI Audit" template: a 1-page document that analyses a business's online presence and suggests 3 AI improvements. Complete one audit for a real local business as practice.
  • Draft 2 cold outreach messages (one email, one LinkedIn DM) that lead with value, reference the audit, and propose a 15-minute call. No selling — just offering insights.

Pro Tip: The best clients are not searching for "AI services." They are Googling "how to respond to customers faster" or "why is nobody visiting my website." Solve the pain, not the technology.

10 Day

The Outreach Sprint

Module 5 — Finding & Landing Clients 45 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Execute a structured outreach campaign: send 10 personalised messages in one session
  • Handle common objections ("We already tried AI," "It is too expensive," "We are too small")
  • Set up a simple CRM or spreadsheet to track your outreach pipeline

Practice Tasks

  • Send 10 personalised outreach messages (email or LinkedIn) to businesses from your Day 9 list. Each message must reference something specific about their business — no copy-paste templates.
  • Create a tracking spreadsheet with columns: Business Name, Contact, Channel, Date Sent, Response, Follow-Up Date, Status. Log all 10 contacts.
  • Write 3 "objection scripts" — short, confident responses to: "AI is just a fad," "We cannot afford it," and "We tried a chatbot and it was terrible." Practice saying them out loud.

Pro Tip: Most people quit after 3 messages with no reply. The data shows it takes 5-7 touchpoints to get a response. Persistence is not annoying — it is professional. Follow up.

11 Day

Price Like a Pro

Module 6 — Pricing & Packaging 45 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Understand value-based pricing vs hourly rates (and why hourly kills your income)
  • Create 3 service packages at different price points (Starter, Growth, Premium)
  • Learn the psychology of pricing anchoring and how to make your middle tier irresistible

Practice Tasks

  • Design your 3-tier pricing menu for your primary AI service. Include: package name, what is included, what is excluded, price, and a one-line value statement. Make the middle tier the obvious choice.
  • Create a 1-page "Service Proposal" template in Canva or Google Docs. Include: client name, problem summary, proposed solution, package options, timeline, and next steps. This is what you send after a discovery call.
  • Role-play a pricing conversation with ChatGPT: prompt it to act as a sceptical business owner asking about costs. Practice justifying your price by referencing ROI, not hours worked.

Pro Tip: Never say "It costs £500." Say "This system will save you £2,000/month in staff time — the investment is £500 to set it up." Same price, completely different conversation.

12 Day

Client Delivery Systems

Module 7 — Delivery & Operations 50 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Set up a professional onboarding process that makes clients feel confident from day one
  • Create SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) for your core services so delivery is consistent
  • Build a client communication system with regular updates and clear milestones

Practice Tasks

  • Create a "Client Welcome Kit" document: includes a welcome message, project timeline, what you need from them (logins, brand assets, etc.), communication channels, and FAQ. Make it look professional.
  • Write an SOP for your most complex service (e.g., chatbot setup). Break it into numbered steps from kickoff to handover. Include estimated time per step. This is your delivery playbook.
  • Set up a free project management tool (Trello, Notion, or ClickUp). Create a template board/page for client projects with stages: Onboarding, In Progress, Review, Delivered, Follow-Up.

Pro Tip: The number one reason freelancers lose clients is not bad work — it is bad communication. A weekly 2-minute update email keeps clients happy, even when you are still working on their project.

13 Day

Recurring Revenue & Retainers

Module 7 — Delivery & Operations 45 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Understand how to turn one-off projects into monthly retainers (the key to predictable income)
  • Design a "maintenance and optimisation" package that clients renew every month
  • Calculate your monthly recurring revenue target and reverse-engineer the client numbers

Practice Tasks

  • Design a monthly retainer offer: "AI Content & Chatbot Management" (or similar). Define exactly what is included monthly: number of content pieces, chatbot updates, performance reports, response time SLA.
  • Create a "Month 1 Report" template that you would send a retainer client. Include: metrics overview, what was delivered, optimisations made, and recommendations for next month.
  • Do the maths: If your retainer is £500/month, how many clients do you need for £5K/month? £10K/month? Write down your 90-day client acquisition target.

Pro Tip: 10 retainer clients at £500/month = £5,000/month = £60,000/year. That is a full-time income from a laptop. The goal is not more clients — it is the right clients who stay.

14 Day

Launch Day: Your AI Business is Live

Module 8 — Scaling Your AI Business 60 mins

Learning Objectives

  • Finalise and publish all assets: portfolio, pricing, outreach templates, and delivery systems
  • Create a 30-day action plan with weekly revenue targets and daily habits
  • Understand the scaling levers: hiring, automating delivery, raising prices, and adding services

Practice Tasks

  • Publish your portfolio (make it live). Update your LinkedIn headline to reflect your AI services positioning. Post your first piece of content about AI for business (a tip, insight, or case study).
  • Write your "30-Day Launch Plan" on one page: Week 1 goals (outreach targets), Week 2 (first client), Week 3 (deliver + get testimonial), Week 4 (refine + scale). Pin it where you will see it daily.
  • Send 5 more outreach messages today. Follow up with anyone from Day 10 who has not responded. Book at least 1 discovery call this week. Your business starts now — not after "one more course."

Pro Tip: "The best time to start was yesterday. The second best time is now." You have the skills, the tools, and the plan. The only thing between you and £10K/month is consistent action. Go build it.
