How to Use This Plan

This is your personal presenter preparation plan. Work through one day at a time, spending 30-45 minutes on each session. Every day builds on the last, taking you from practising your opening hook through live demos to full run-throughs. Tick off each task as you complete it and track your daily progress below. By Day 14, you will walk into that workshop room feeling prepared, polished, and genuinely confident.

Week 1 — Content & Live Demos
01 Day

Your Opening (The Hook)

Public Speaking 30 mins
Practice saying this out loud 5 times: "How many of you spent time this week writing emails, creating social media posts, or chasing leads? What if I told you that by the end of today, you'll see how AI can do all of that for you while you sleep?"

Practice Tasks

  • Stand in front of a mirror
  • Say it slowly, make eye contact with yourself
  • Try different tones — excited, calm, curious
  • Record yourself on your phone and watch it back

Goal: Sound natural, not rehearsed

02 Day

The Wake Up Call Stats

Data & Urgency 30 mins

Practice presenting these facts with conviction and pause:

  • "40% of businesses will fail in the next 5 years if they don't adopt AI"
  • "Companies using AI agents are growing 3x faster than those who don't"
  • "The AI industry is worth 200 billion and growing every single day"

Practice Tasks

  • Practice pausing after each stat. Let it sink in.
  • Try saying "Let that sink in" after the biggest stat

Goal: Create urgency without sounding scary

03 Day

Storytelling Practice

Narrative & Connection 35 mins

Write out 3 short stories (real or realistic):

Story 1: A small business owner drowning in admin work. Found AI. Now has free time.
Story 2: Someone with no tech skills who built an AI system in a weekend.
Story 3: A side hustler who replaced their 9-5 income using AI agents.

Practice Tasks

  • Write out Story 1 in full (under 2 minutes when spoken)
  • Write out Story 2 in full (under 2 minutes when spoken)
  • Write out Story 3 in full (under 2 minutes when spoken)
  • Practice telling each story aloud — time yourself

Goal: Make the audience see themselves in the story

04 Day

The Live Demo — Part 1 (AI Image Generation)

Live Demo 35 mins

Practice Tasks

  • Open WavesAI Studio on your laptop
  • Practice generating an image with a prompt like "professional product photo of luxury candle on marble table"
  • Time yourself — the generation takes about 15-20 seconds
  • While it generates, practice saying: "In the time it takes to make a coffee, AI just created a professional image that would cost you 200 pounds from a photographer"
  • Do this 3 times until it feels smooth

Goal: No fumbling, confident clicking

05 Day

The Live Demo — Part 2 (AI Content)

Live Demo 30 mins

Practice Tasks

  • Practice generating a blog post or marketing email using AI
  • Pick a simple prompt like: "Write a marketing email for a local bakery announcing a new sourdough bread"
  • Practice your reaction: "Look at that. A full professional email in 10 seconds. Now imagine doing this for 50 clients."
  • Run through the full demo sequence 3 times

Goal: Show speed and quality of AI output

Workshop Highlight — The WOW Moment
06 Day

The Live Demo — Part 3 (Building an AI Agent Live)

Live Demo — Showstopper 45 mins

This is the highlight of the workshop! Practice building a Customer Service AI Agent live with the audience.

Practice Tasks

  • Open Claude AI and practice the step-by-step agent build
  • Step 1: Define the agent's role and personality (2 mins)
  • Step 2: Feed it a business FAQ / product list (2 mins)
  • Step 3: Add conversation rules — greetings, objection handling, human handoff (3 mins)
  • Step 4: Test it live — ask it questions and show the audience the responses (5 mins)
  • Practice saying: "We just built a working AI customer service agent in 12 minutes. Businesses pay 500 to 2,000 pounds for this."
  • Do the full build 3 times until it feels effortless

Goal: The agent build is the WOW moment — make it smooth and impressive

07 Day

Rest Day

Recovery & Reflection No timer

Don't practice anything today. Let the first week of preparation settle in your mind. Your brain processes and consolidates learning during rest — trust the process.

Suggestions

  • Just think about the workshop casually
  • Watch an inspiring AI presentation on YouTube
  • Let everything settle in your mind

Goal: Recharge so you come back sharp for Week 2

Week 2 — Polish & Full Run-Throughs
08 Day

The 8 AI Agents Overview

Product Knowledge 40 mins

Practice a 2-minute pitch for each agent type. For each one, have one example result ready.

1. Lead Generation Agent "Finds customers while you sleep"
2. Content Agent "Creates weeks of content in minutes"
3. Voice Agent "Answers your phone 24/7 professionally"
4. Video Agent "Makes video ads without a camera crew"
5. SEO Agent "Gets you found on Google automatically"
6. Email Agent "Sends personalised emails at scale"
7. Chat Agent "Handles customer questions instantly"
8. Sales Agent "Follows up and books meetings for you"

Practice Tasks

  • Write a 2-minute pitch for each of the 8 agents
  • For each agent, prepare one example result (e.g. "This agent helped a plumber get 15 new clients in one month")
  • Practice delivering all 8 pitches back to back

Goal: Quick, punchy, impressive

09 Day

The Interactive Exercise

Audience Engagement 30 mins

Practice Tasks

  • Practice giving clear instructions to a room: "Everyone take out your phone. Open your browser. Go to chat.openai.com. Type this exact prompt..."
  • Practice reading the prompt slowly and clearly
  • Practice walking around pretending to help people (builds confidence moving in the space)

Goal: Feel comfortable leading a group activity

10 Day

The Offer and Close

Sales & Conversion 35 mins

This is the money moment. Practice each of these lines until they feel natural:

The Bridge: "Everything I showed you today — the image generation, the content creation, the AI agent we built together, all 8 AI systems — I teach every single one step by step in my course."
The Pricing Reveal: "The course normally costs 297 pounds. But because you showed up today and invested your time, everyone in this room gets it for [workshop price]."
The Urgency: "This price is only available today, right now, in this room."
The QR Code Moment: "Take out your phone, scan this code, and you'll be inside the course in 60 seconds."

Practice Tasks

  • Practice the full close sequence from bridge to QR code
  • Say each line out loud at least 3 times
  • Practice the pricing reveal with confident body language
  • Time the full close — aim for 3-4 minutes total

Goal: Sound confident about the value, not salesy

11 Day

Handling Questions

Objection Handling 30 mins

Practice confident, quick answers to the most common audience questions:

  • Q: "Do I need to be technical?"
    A: "Absolutely not. The course is designed for complete beginners. If you can use a smartphone, you can do this."
  • Q: "How long does it take?"
    A: "Most students complete it in 4-6 weeks doing just 30 minutes a day."
  • Q: "Can I really make money with this?"
    A: "The course teaches you how to sell AI services to businesses. Plus with resell rights, you can sell the course itself and keep 50%."
  • Q: "What if it doesn't work for me?"
    A: "30-day money back guarantee. No questions asked."

Practice Tasks

  • Read through each Q&A pair out loud
  • Practice answering without reading — from memory
  • Have someone quiz you with random questions from the list
  • Practice maintaining eye contact while answering

Goal: Have confident, quick answers ready

12 Day

Full Run-Through (Sitting Down)

Full Rehearsal 90 mins

Practice Tasks

  • Do the entire 90-minute workshop sitting at your desk
  • Use the slides on your screen
  • Talk through every part out loud (including the live agent build)
  • Don't worry about mistakes, just get through it all
  • Time yourself — note the total duration

Goal: Know the flow from start to finish

13 Day

Full Run-Through (Standing Up)

Full Rehearsal 90 mins

Practice Tasks

  • Do it again but standing up this time
  • If you have a family member or friend, ask them to watch
  • Practice your body language — hands visible, not in pockets
  • Practice moving around a little, not standing frozen
  • Include the live agent build — do it standing with the laptop

Goal: Feel the physical experience of presenting

14 Day

Final Polish

Final Preparation 45 mins

Practice Tasks

  • Do one last run-through focusing only on your weak spots
  • Review your speaker notes one more time
  • Test all your tech (laptop, slides, WavesAI Studio login, Claude AI login, internet connection)
  • Practice the agent build one more time — this is your showstopper
  • Charge your laptop fully
  • Get a good night's sleep

Goal: Walk in tomorrow feeling prepared and calm

Workshop Day Checklist

The morning of your workshop — tick these off before you begin

  • Arrive 30 minutes early to set up
  • Test the projector/screen connection
  • Have a glass of water ready
  • Pre-open WavesAI Studio and Claude AI in browser tabs
  • Have the agent build prompts ready in a notes app (backup)
  • Take 5 deep breaths before you start
  • Remember: you know more about AI than everyone in that room
  • Smile, be yourself, and have fun with it
You Are Ready

14 Days of Preparation Complete

You have practised every section, rehearsed every demo, and run through the entire workshop twice. You are not winging it — you are walking in prepared. Trust your preparation, trust yourself, and go deliver a workshop they will never forget.
