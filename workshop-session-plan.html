Opening — The Hook
0:00

The Hook

Energy, Urgency, Connection

Welcome and Energy Setter (0:00 - 2:00) — Walk in with energy. Smile. Make eye contact with people in the front row. Start before everyone is fully settled.

Say This (The Hook Question) "How many of you spent time this week writing emails, creating social media posts, or chasing leads? Raise your hand. Yeah, me too. Now — what if I told you that by the end of today, you'll see how AI can do all of that for you while you sleep?"

The Wake-Up Call (2:00 - 5:00) — Pause after each stat. Let the numbers land. Make eye contact with someone different for each one.

  • "40% of businesses will fail in the next 5 years if they don't adopt AI. Forty percent."
  • "The AI industry is worth 200 billion pounds right now — and it's growing every single day."
  • "Companies using AI are growing 3x faster than those who don't. Three times."

Three Short Stories (5:00 - 9:00) — Tell each story in under 90 seconds. Make them feel real and relatable.

Story 1 — The Business Owner A small business owner was drowning in admin. Spending every evening writing emails, chasing invoices, posting on social media. She discovered AI, set up a few simple systems, and within a month she had her evenings back. Her business didn't just survive — it grew.
Story 2 — The Non-Technical Person A man with no coding experience, no technical background at all, sat down on a Saturday morning and by Sunday evening he had built a working AI customer service system. No programming. No developer fees. Just him, a laptop, and the right guidance.
Story 3 — The Side Hustler Someone started offering AI services to local businesses as a side hustle. Within three months, they were earning more from AI services than their 9-to-5 job. They quit. And they haven't looked back.
Transition (9:00 - 10:00) "Those aren't fairy tales. Those are real results from real people using exactly the tools I'm about to show you. Let me show you exactly what I mean..."
Key Point The goal of the opening is NOT to teach. It's to make the audience lean forward and think "I need to pay attention to this."

Energy Check: By minute 10, the room should feel awake, curious, and slightly nervous about missing out.

Live Demo 1 — AI Image Generation
10:00
25:00 15 min

AI Image Generation

First WOW Moment — Visual Impact

Step-by-Step Demo Flow

  • Open WavesAI Studio live on screen. Let the audience see the interface. Don't rush — let them take it in.
  • Type the prompt: "professional product photo of luxury candle on marble table"
  • Click generate. The image takes 15-20 seconds. This is your moment to talk.
Say This While It Generates "Right now, AI is creating a professional product image from scratch. No photographer, no studio, no lights, no editing. In the time it takes to make a coffee, AI just created a professional image that would cost you 200 pounds from a photographer."

Show the result. React naturally. Point out the quality — the lighting, the composition, the realism. Let the audience react too.

Audience Participation Image

  • Ask the audience: "Right, someone give me a product. Any product. A shoe, a perfume bottle, a pizza — anything."
  • Take a suggestion and type it in as a new prompt live
  • While it generates, build on the energy: "Watch this — your product idea, generated in seconds"
  • Show the result. Ask the person who suggested it: "Would you use this on your website?"
Key Point "This is just ONE of 8 AI systems you can offer to businesses. We've only been going 15 minutes and you've already seen something most business owners have never seen."

Energy Check: Audience should be visibly impressed. You should hear reactions — "wow", "no way", whispers to neighbours.

Live Demo 2 — AI Content & Voice
25:00
40:00 15 min

AI Content & Voice

Speed & Scale — The Money Demo

Marketing Email Demo (25:00 - 30:00)

Step-by-Step Demo Flow

  • Open the content generation tool. Type the prompt: "Write a marketing email for a local bakery announcing a new sourdough bread"
  • Hit generate. The result appears in seconds.
  • Read a few lines of the email out loud to the audience. Point out the subject line, the call to action, the professional tone.
Say This "A full professional marketing email in 10 seconds. Think about that. Now imagine doing this for 50 clients. For 100 clients. Each one gets a unique, personalised email and you didn't write a single word."

Voiceover Demo (30:00 - 35:00) — Switch to the Text-to-Speech tool.

Voice Generation

  • Take a line from the email you just generated and paste it into the TTS tool
  • Select a professional voice. Generate the audio.
  • Play it for the room. Let them hear how natural it sounds.
Say This "That's not a human voice artist. That's AI. No recording studio, no voice talent fees, no scheduling. You type the words, AI speaks them. Professionally. In any language."

Video Editor Tease (35:00 - 40:00) — Briefly show the Video Editor combining visuals and voice.

Quick Show

  • Show how the image from Demo 1 and the voiceover can be combined into a video ad
  • Don't do a full video build — just show the interface and a pre-made example if possible
  • The point is: "These tools work together. One flows into the next."
Key Point "Businesses pay 500 to 2,000 pounds for this kind of content — the email, the voiceover, the video. You just watched AI create it all in under 5 minutes."

Energy Check: The audience should now be thinking about money. "I could charge for this." That's exactly the thought you want.

Live Demo 3 — AI Automation
40:00

AI Automation Overview

The "While You Sleep" Proof

Step-by-Step Demo Flow

  • Open Make.com (or similar automation tool) and show a simple workflow on screen
  • Walk through the flow visually: "A customer fills in this form on a website..."
  • Step 1: Form submitted
  • Step 2: Customer automatically added to a database
  • Step 3: Welcome email sent instantly
  • Step 4: Follow-up email scheduled for 3 days later
  • Step 5: Notification sent to the business owner
  • Point at each step as you explain it. Make it visual and easy to follow.
Say This "This entire workflow runs automatically. No human involved. A customer fills in a form at 2am, and by the time the business owner wakes up, the customer has already received a welcome email, been added to the system, and has a follow-up scheduled. Set it up once — it works 24/7 without you."
Connect It Back "Remember the opening question? Writing emails, creating content, chasing leads. This is how you stop doing all of that manually. You build these systems once, and they run forever."
Key Point "Set it up once, it works 24/7 without you. This is not a tool — it's a business that runs itself."

Energy Check: By now, audience members should be imagining their own business running on autopilot. Some will be taking photos of the screen.

Interactive Exercise — Let Them Try
60:00
70:00 10 min

Let Them Try

Hands-On — Personal Experience
Say This (Clear Instructions) "Alright, enough watching me. It's your turn. Everyone take out your phone right now. Open your browser. Go to chat.openai.com — or if you prefer, claude.ai. Either one works."

Wait for everyone to get their phones out. Walk to the centre of the room. Be patient. Give them 30-60 seconds.

The Prompt to Give Them

  • Put this prompt on the screen (large text):
  • "Write a professional Instagram caption for a local coffee shop promoting their new summer menu. Include 3 relevant hashtags."
  • Read it out slowly and clearly. Spell out the URL if needed.
  • Say: "Type that in exactly as you see it, and hit enter."

While They Try (Walk Around)

  • Walk around the room. Look over shoulders. Help people who are struggling.
  • Point out good results: "Look at that one — that's Instagram-ready right now."
  • Let the excitement build organically. People will show each other.
  • Give them about 3-4 minutes to play with it.
Bring Them Back "Amazing, right? Now here's the thing — you just created professional marketing content on your phone in 30 seconds. For free. Now imagine being able to offer this as a service to businesses. Imagine charging 200, 500, even 1,000 pounds a month to do this for companies who don't know how."
Key Point "Now imagine being able to offer this as a service to businesses. That free tool on your phone just became a business."

Energy Check: The room should be buzzing. People talking to each other, showing each other their screens. This is the highest-energy moment of the session.

The Offer

Sales & Conversion — The Money Moment

The Bridge (70:00 - 72:00) — Transition smoothly from the exercise energy into the offer. Don't change your tone dramatically — stay conversational.

The Bridge "Everything I showed you today — the image generation, the content creation, the automation, the voiceover, all 8 AI systems — I teach every single one step by step in my course. Not theory. Not slides. You sit down, you follow along, and you build."

Course Overview (72:00 - 75:00) — Keep this factual and confident. Not salesy.

What's In The Course

  • 8 complete modules covering every AI agent system you saw today
  • 43 video lessons — short, practical, no fluff
  • Built-in AI tools you can use immediately
  • A professional certificate when you complete it

The Two Agent Builds (75:00 - 77:00) — This is the differentiator. Emphasise "build", not "learn about".

The Builds "By the middle of the course, you'll have built a working Customer Service AI Agent — the kind of thing businesses are paying thousands for right now. By the end, you'll have built a Social Media Management Agent. Both are real products. Both are sellable. Both are yours."

The Pricing Reveal (77:00 - 79:00) — Pause before you say the price. Let the anticipation build.

The Price "The course normally costs 297 pounds. That's less than what one single client would pay you to build one of these agents. But because you showed up today, because you invested your time and energy to be in this room... everyone here gets it for [workshop price]."

The Urgency (79:00 - 80:00) — Say this calmly but firmly. Don't shout it.

Urgency "This price is only available today. Right now. In this room. When you walk out of that door, it goes back to full price. This is a one-time offer for the people who showed up."

The MRR Mention (80:00 - 81:00) — Plant the resell seed.

Resell Rights "And if you go for the Business tier — you don't just get the course. You get the right to resell it. That means you can sell this exact course to your own audience, your own network, and keep 50% of every single sale. That's recurring income from a product you didn't have to build."

The QR Code (81:00 - 82:00) — Put it on screen NOW. Make it huge.

The CTA "Take out your phone. Scan this code. You'll be inside the course in 60 seconds. No complicated sign-up. Just scan, pay, and you're in."
Key Point Stay calm and confident during the close. You are offering value, not begging for a sale. The demos already did the selling — you are just opening the door.

Energy Check: Phones should be coming out. You should see people scanning. Don't rush to Q&A — let the QR code sit on screen.

Q&A — Handle Questions & Final Push
82:00
90:00 8 min

Q&A and Final Push

Objection Handling & Last Conversions
Open the Q&A "Before we wrap up — who has questions? About AI, about the course, about anything you saw today. No question is too simple."

Keep the QR code on screen the entire time. Every question answered is another chance for someone to decide to buy.

  • Q: "Do I need to be technical?"
    A: "Absolutely not. If you can use a smartphone, you can do this. The course is designed for complete beginners. I'll walk you through every single click."
  • Q: "How long does it take?"
    A: "Most students complete it in 4 to 6 weeks doing just 30 minutes a day. But you can go at your own pace — once you're in, you have lifetime access."
  • Q: "Can I really make money with this?"
    A: "The course teaches you to sell AI services to businesses. Every business needs this. Plus with the resell rights on the Business tier — you can sell the course itself and keep 50% of every sale. That's two income streams from one purchase."
  • Q: "What if it doesn't work for me?"
    A: "30-day money back guarantee. No questions asked. If you're not happy, you get every penny back. There's literally zero risk."
  • Q: "Is AI going to replace my job?"
    A: "AI won't replace you — but someone who knows how to use AI might. That's exactly why you should be the person who knows how to use it. Be the one offering AI services, not the one being replaced by them."
  • Q: "I don't have a business. Is this still useful?"
    A: "Absolutely. You can start an AI services business from scratch. Or use the resell rights to sell the course. You don't need an existing business — AI is the business."
Final Push (Last 60 Seconds) "The QR code is still on the screen. This is the last chance at this price. Once we leave this room, it's gone. If anything you saw today made you think 'I want to learn how to do that' — now is the time. Scan the code. You'll be inside the course before you get to your car."
Thank You and Close "Thank you all for being here today. Thank you for your time, your energy, and your curiosity. Whatever you decide, I hope today showed you what's possible with AI. For those of you joining the course — I'll see you inside. Let's go build something amazing."
Key Point Never end on a question. Always end on YOUR terms — with the CTA and a strong final line. Leave them inspired, not confused.

After the Session: Stay in the room for 10-15 minutes. People will come up to you with questions. This is where you close the people who were "almost ready". Be warm, be patient, and have the QR code ready on your phone.
